# The DSR

The daily status report is the list of what you worked on, with hours.

## What fills in by itself

When a ticket you are assigned to is resolved or closed, an entry appears for
that day. Auto entries are tagged **Auto**.

## What you add by hand

Under **From your work today** you will see tickets you touched with a guess at
the hours. Check the number and press **Add**.

For anything else press **Add entry**, write what you did, pick a category and
enter the hours. The 0.5h, 1h, 2h and 4h buttons fill the hours field.

## Changing an entry

Each row saves itself when you change a field. Edit the task, category, hours,
status or note and the row updates in place. Status colors the bar on the left:
green for completed, blue for in progress, red for blocked.

## Who can edit what

A day accepts entries until midnight local time. After that it is read-only and
says why. Future days are closed for everyone. Owners and admins can still fix a
past day, open anyone's report from the employee picker, and see the team
overview under the log.

**Copy DSR text** puts a plain summary on your clipboard for Slack or Teams.
