# The DSR

The daily status report is the list of what you worked on, with hours. It is one table of everything submitted, grouped by day.

## Reading it

Switch between **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days** and **Last 30 days**, and search by task, note or product. Each day shows its total and
how many entries it holds. Owners and admins can pick a person, or **Everyone**, from the list next to the range. **Export CSV** saves what you are looking at
as a spreadsheet, and **Copy summary** puts a plain summary on your clipboard for Slack or Teams.

## What fills in by itself

When a ticket you are assigned to is resolved or closed, an entry appears for that day, tagged **Auto**. It shows when the ticket was created and completed.

**A ticket that was open a long time** (more than a working day) does not claim that time. Its row says "open for 11d 1h (not counted)" and counts nothing
until you enter how long you actually worked on it today. When you resolve such a ticket a message tells you, the DSR opens a small window for it, and a banner
with an **Add time** button stays until you do. Only the time you enter counts.

## What you add by hand

Press **Log work**, write what you did, pick the project (or leave it on "No product" for meetings), a category, the time (for example `1h 30m`) and a status.
You only see products you can open.

When you touched tickets today that are not in your DSR yet, a banner lists them. **Add all** logs them at suggested hours, or **Review** lets you change each time first.

## Changing an entry

The pencil opens the entry to edit, and the bin deletes it (it asks first). Status colours the badge: green for completed, blue for in progress, red for blocked.

## Who can edit what

A day accepts entries until midnight local time. After that it is read-only. Future days are closed for everyone. Owners and admins can still fix a past day and open
anyone's report.
