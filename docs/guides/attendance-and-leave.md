# Attendance and leave

## Punching in and out

Open **My attendance** and press **Check in** when you start. Before that the card
shows the time. After it, the card counts down the time left in your full day,
second by second, and says when you finish. A full day is the scheduled working
hours plus the unpaid break, counted from your actual check-in, so a late start
moves the finish time later. Press **Check out** when you leave. A second punch within 45
seconds is ignored, so a double click does not undo itself.

The week strip shows how much of each day you worked. The strip of numbers
below it covers today and the month you are viewing, including hours worked this
month and the daily average.

**The month grid** shows one square per day. Dark green is a full day (at least 90%
of the scheduled hours), light green a part day, blue the day you are on the clock,
orange a day with no check-out, and an outlined square a working day with no punch.
Hover a square for its hours. The list beside it counts each kind.

## Weekly off days

Saturday and Sunday are weekly offs unless your company says otherwise. An owner
or admin changes them with the **Weekly off** button on **Team attendance**.

On a weekly off nobody is expected in, so none of the shift rules apply. You can
still check in and out, on Home or on My attendance, and work any hours you
like. You are never late, there is no grace period, penalty or half day, the
day is not short or absent, and it does not move your pay. The punch card says
"Weekly off" instead of counting down a working day.

Payroll and leave use the same days. A leave request is charged only on working
days, and a pay cycle's working days are the days that are not weekly offs or
holidays. Changing the days applies from then on: a pay run that is already
locked stays as it was, and an unlocked one picks the new days up when you
generate it again.

## A wrong or forgotten punch

Use **Request a correction**. Pick the day, enter the right times and give a
reason. An owner or admin approves or rejects it and you get a notification.
Rules:

* The day must be within the last 60 days.
* One open request per day.
* Nobody decides their own request unless they are the only owner or admin.

## Leave

**Request leave** shows who else is off on those days and which day is busiest,
so you can pick a quieter week. The **Leave calendar** shows the whole team by
day. Company holidays are marked, and requests that still wait for a decision
are dashed.

Owners and admins approve from **Leave approvals**, where the same clash
warning appears next to each request.
