# Attendance and leave

## Punching in and out

Open **My attendance** and press **Check in** when you start. The ring and the
counter run live. Press **Check out** when you leave. A second punch within 45
seconds is ignored, so a double click does not undo itself.

The week strip shows how much of each day you worked. The strip of numbers
below it covers today and the month you are viewing.

## A wrong or forgotten punch

Use **Request a correction**. Pick the day, enter the right times and give a
reason. An owner or admin approves or rejects it and you get a notification.
Rules:

* The day must be within the last 60 days.
* One open request per day.
* Nobody decides their own request unless they are the only owner or admin.

## Leave

**Request leave** shows who else is off on those days and which day is busiest,
so you can pick a quieter week. The **Leave calendar** shows the whole team by
day. Company holidays are marked, and requests that still wait for a decision
are dashed.

Owners and admins approve from **Leave approvals**, where the same clash
warning appears next to each request.
