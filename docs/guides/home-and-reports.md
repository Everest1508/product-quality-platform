# Home and reports

## Home

The top tile greets you and shows the time from your own device, so it is right
wherever you are. The line under the greeting changes daily. Press the arrow
next to it for another one. That swaps only the line, not the page.

Below it are your tiles: today's punch, pay, leave balances, your DSR week,
tickets assigned to you. Owners and admins also see leave waiting for approval
and company totals.

## Reports

Pick a range with the buttons (Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, Last 30 days,
This month) or set exact dates. The page updates without reloading.

* The five tiles at the top compare each number with the same number of days
  just before. A green arrow up is more than before, a red arrow down is less.
  "new" means there was nothing in the earlier period.
* **Day by day** shows tickets created and resolved, and errors captured and
  resolved, for each day. Hover a day for its numbers.
* **How much got finished** is resolved divided by created, for tickets and for
  errors.
* **Busiest products** adds tickets, errors and feedback per product.
* **What happened most** ranks the kinds of events in the audit log.
* The two tables underneath hold the exact numbers by product and by day.

Reports are built from the audit log. Owners and admins see the whole company.
Everyone else sees only what they did themselves, on products they can open.
