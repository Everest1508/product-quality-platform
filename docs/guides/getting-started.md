# Getting started

## Sign in and pick a workspace

Sign in with your email and password. If you belong to more than one company,
the switcher under the logo changes which one you are looking at. Everything on
the page, including the sidebar counts, belongs to the active company.

## What you can see

Owners and admins see every product. Everyone else sees only the products they
were given access to. A ticket or error from a product you cannot open never
shows up in a list, in search, or in a count.

## Find things fast

* Press **Ctrl K** (Cmd K on a Mac) or **/** to search tickets, errors, products and pages.
  Typing a ticket number jumps straight to it.
* The bell in the sidebar lists what is waiting for you: assignments, mentions,
  decisions on your leave.
* Collapse the sidebar with the arrow at the bottom. Hover any icon to see its name.
  A blue dot on an icon means something is waiting there.

## Install it on your phone

Open the site in Chrome or Safari and choose "Add to Home Screen". The app
opens full screen and shows an offline page if the connection drops. It never
saves a change while you are offline.
