# Domain playbooks — what good looks like by kind of product

Use with the Screen brief. Each playbook: who uses it and where, the screens that matter most, what good looks like, style fits (from `styles.md`), and common pitfalls. These are starting points — the product's own identity and brand still decide.

---

## HR / HRMS / payroll
- **Users:** employees (occasional, mobile), managers (approvals), HR/admin (daily, desktop, dense).
- **Key screens:** my day/home, attendance & punch in/out, leave apply/approve, payslips, employee profile, org chart, reports.
- **Good:** employee screens are friendly and fast (punch in = one tap, leave balance at a glance); manager screens put approvals first; HR screens are dense tables with filters and bulk actions; sensitive data (salary, documents) clearly protected.
- **Style fits:** Corporate trust or Clinical calm for admin; Soft & friendly or Playful for employee home/engagement.
- **Pitfalls:** same dense UI for employees and HR; approvals buried; calendar logic unclear (weekly offs, holidays, half days).

## ERP / manufacturing / inventory
- **Users:** planners, storekeepers, supervisors, accounts — daily, desktop; shop floor — tablets.
- **Key screens:** orders/jobs, BOM, stock, purchase, production status, quality, dispatch.
- **Good:** dense tables, keyboard entry, copy-from-previous, codes shown with names, status always visible, traceability (who/when).
- **Style fits:** Dense pro (office), Industrial (floor), Dark command center (production monitoring).
- **Pitfalls:** desktop-only layouts used on tablets; codes without names; long forms without sections or defaults.

## Restaurant / POS / food
- **Users:** cashiers and waiters (fast, standing, touch), kitchen (glanceable at distance), owners (reports).
- **Key screens:** order entry, table map, KOT/kitchen display, billing, menu management, day close.
- **Good:** huge touch targets, menu as visual grid by category, order summary always visible, one-tap common actions, kitchen screens readable from 2m with timers and colour by age.
- **Style fits:** Warm hospitality (customer-facing, menus) × Dense pro/Industrial (POS, kitchen).
- **Pitfalls:** small targets, modal-heavy flows, nested menus for modifiers.

## Salon / spa / bookings
- **Users:** front desk (busy, interrupted), staff (their schedule), customers (mobile booking).
- **Key screens:** calendar by staff/chair, booking, client profile & history, services & pricing, checkout, packages/memberships.
- **Good:** calendar is the hero, drag to reschedule, conflicts shown instantly, client history one click away, reminders visible.
- **Style fits:** Warm hospitality, Premium minimal (high-end), Soft & friendly (customer app).
- **Pitfalls:** list-based scheduling, hidden staff availability, checkout separate from booking.

## CRM / sales / marketing
- **Users:** sales reps (daily, mobile + desktop), managers (pipeline, forecasts), marketing (campaigns).
- **Key screens:** pipeline board, lead/contact detail, activity timeline, tasks/follow-ups, campaign dashboards.
- **Good:** next action always visible, one-click log call/email, kanban pipeline with values per stage, timeline-led detail page.
- **Style fits:** Swiss functional, Soft & friendly, Neo-brutalist (startup teams).
- **Pitfalls:** data-entry-heavy forms before any value, no clear "what should I do today".

## Finance / accounting / billing
- **Users:** accountants (expert, daily), owners (summaries), customers (invoices).
- **Key screens:** ledgers, invoices, reconciliation, payments, tax reports, cash flow.
- **Good:** tabular numbers, right-aligned, clear debit/credit and sign, totals sticky, audit trail, export/print views that match documents, no ambiguity in dates and currency.
- **Style fits:** Corporate trust, Dense pro, Editorial (reports).
- **Pitfalls:** colour-only negatives, rounding inconsistencies, hidden filters affecting totals.

## Healthcare / pharma / labs
- **Users:** clinicians (time-poor, high stakes), lab/QA staff (procedural), patients (anxious, occasional).
- **Key screens:** patient/sample record, orders, results with ranges, schedules, compliance logs, batch records.
- **Good:** legibility first, units always shown, abnormal values flagged with text and icon, confirmations for irreversible actions, audit trails, no clutter.
- **Style fits:** Clinical calm, Corporate trust.
- **Pitfalls:** dense colour coding without labels, truncated names/IDs, ambiguous dates.

## E-commerce (admin & storefront)
- **Users:** shoppers (mobile, impatient), store staff (orders, catalogue), owners.
- **Good storefront:** product imagery first, price and delivery clear, one CTA, frictionless checkout (guest, autofill, wallet).
- **Good admin:** order queue by status, bulk fulfilment, catalogue grid with quick edit, low-stock alerts.
- **Style fits:** brand-led storefront (any style that fits the brand); Dense pro or Swiss for admin.
- **Pitfalls:** checkout forms with unnecessary fields, admin tables without filters.

## Education / learning
- **Users:** learners (motivation matters), teachers (grading, tracking), admins.
- **Good:** progress and next lesson front and centre, encouraging feedback, clear deadlines, teacher views with bulk grading.
- **Style fits:** Soft & friendly, Playful, Editorial (content-heavy).
- **Pitfalls:** walls of text, unclear progress, punitive tone.

## Logistics / field service
- **Users:** dispatchers (map + list), drivers/technicians (phone, outdoors, gloves), customers (tracking).
- **Good:** map + list split view, status timeline, one-tap status updates, offline tolerance, big targets, photo/signature capture.
- **Style fits:** Industrial (field app), Dark command center or Dense pro (dispatch).
- **Pitfalls:** tiny controls on mobile, no offline state, status changes needing many taps.

## Monitoring / IoT / energy
- **Users:** operators watching for long periods, engineers diagnosing, managers reviewing.
- **Good:** live values big and labelled with units and last-updated time, thresholds drawn on charts, alarm states unmistakable, history one click away, works on wall displays.
- **Style fits:** Dark command center, Clinical calm (reports), Industrial (panel displays).
- **Pitfalls:** stale data without warning, alarms that look like normal values, too many gauges.

## Real estate / property
- **Good:** imagery-led listings, map search, comparison, clear pricing and availability, enquiry/visit booking.
- **Style fits:** Premium minimal, Warm hospitality, Editorial.

## Internal tools / admin panels
- **Good:** fast search, saved filters, bulk actions, keyboard shortcuts, clear permissions, audit log.
- **Style fits:** Dense pro, Swiss functional, Neo-brutalist (if the team wants personality).

## Marketing / company websites
- **Good:** one clear message above the fold, proof (logos, numbers, case studies), one primary CTA, fast, strong brand expression.
- **Style fits:** whatever expresses the brand — this is where Editorial, Neo-brutalist, Premium minimal and Playful shine.
- **Pitfalls:** generic template hero, stock photos, five CTAs.
